# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and curious learners exploring atomic structure and chemical bonding visually. They need to manipulate atoms directly, compare electron arrangements, and understand why bonds or ions form.

## Product Purpose

Provide an interactive two-dimensional chemistry workspace where users place atoms, inspect shells and subshells, create bonds, and observe how electron structure changes. Success means the visual model makes bonding logic easier to understand than a static diagram or table.

## Brand Personality

Precise, exploratory, and approachable. The interface should feel like a focused scientific workbench without becoming sterile or visually noisy.

## Anti-references

Not a static periodic table, a marketing page, a decorative 3D atom renderer, or a collection of disconnected explanation cards. Avoid hidden electron information, modal-heavy teaching, and controls that separate actions from the canvas.

## Design Principles

- Make the canvas the product.
- Keep electron structure visible while atoms move.
- Teach through direct manipulation and immediate visual feedback.
- Use consistent visual encoding for shells, subshells, charge, and bonds.
- Reveal detail progressively without hiding essential information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, provide keyboard-operable alternatives to drag interactions, never rely on color alone for subshell or bond identity, support reduced motion, and keep labels legible at compact canvas scales.
